Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy covers maxwellian.help and the Maxwellian Drive Connector ("the app"), a Google OAuth application that lets ChatGPT work with files in your Google Drive at your request.

Google user data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, you approve specific Google Drive permissions shown on the Google consent screen. Through those permissions the app can access the Drive file names, metadata and contents needed to carry out a request you make in ChatGPT, such as finding, reading, creating or updating a file. The app does not access your Drive in the background and does not act without a request from you.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the features you ask for in the Drive-to-ChatGPT connection. It is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models by Maxwellian.

Storage and retention

Maxwellian does not store the contents of your Drive files. Your OAuth access is held by the ChatGPT connection you set up, which is operated by OpenAI under its own privacy policy. Maxwellian keeps no copy of your files or Google data beyond what is needed to complete a request in progress.

Sharing and disclosure

Data you ask ChatGPT to read is passed to ChatGPT (OpenAI) as part of your conversation. Maxwellian does not sell Google user data and does not share it with any other third party, except where required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Maxwellian.help's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security

Access is granted through Google's OAuth 2.0 and transmitted over HTTPS. The app never sees your Google password.

Your choices and deletion

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask for deletion of any information Maxwellian holds about you, email the address below and I will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for their use.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated on this page.
