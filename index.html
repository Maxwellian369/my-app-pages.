Maxwellian Drive Connector

What it does. After you sign in with Google and approve access, the connector lets ChatGPT find, read, create and update files in your Google Drive, only when you request it in a ChatGPT conversation.

Who it is for
Individual users who want ChatGPT to work with their own Drive files.
What data it touches
Only the Drive files and metadata needed to complete the request you made. Nothing is stored on Maxwellian servers.
How to stop it
Remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
